A brand with a place in it

From the ponds of Mithila to your pantry.

Mithila Super Foods was founded with a simple mission: preserve the age-old flavours of Mithila and reimagine them for today's health-conscious lifestyle. The brand is built around authenticity, nutrition and respect for local heritage.

Every visual detail — from the lotus-inspired mark to the deep green packaging — is designed to feel rooted without feeling old-fashioned.
